How it works

From registration to result, in four steps

CivicVote keeps the process simple for voters and administrators alike.

01

Register

Create a voter profile with your name, Voter ID, age and email to receive your credentials.

02

Login

Sign in securely with your Voter ID and password to reach your personal dashboard.

03

Cast your vote

Review the candidate list and submit your selection. Each voter may cast exactly one vote.

04

View results

Watch live vote counts and see the declared winner once polls close.

05

Manage your profile

Review your registration details and voting status at any time.

06

Administer elections

Officials can add, edit or remove candidates and open or close the election.
